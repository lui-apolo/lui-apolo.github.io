Recent developments in three-dimensional gravity

BIMSA · Fall 2026

Three-dimensional gravity provides a tractable setting in which questions about the quantum nature of black holes and the structure of holography can be addressed exactly. This course surveys recent developments in the subject, including the holographic dictionary, ensemble interpretations of the gravitational path integral, and conformal bootstrap methods.
