Physics 0

Tsinghua University · Spring 2024

In this course we will study classical mechanics and electrodynamics. The concepts introduced in this class play a fundamental role in the foundations of physical theories and our understanding of the world. These concepts have reverberations throughout physics, and we will illustrate their relationship to more modern developments in the subject.

Schedule

Wednesdays, 09:50–12:15, Teaching Building 4, Room 4302.

Syllabus

  • Kinematics
  • Newton's laws of motion
  • Energy and momentum conservation
  • The harmonic oscillator
  • Newtonian gravitation
  • The Lagrangian formalism
  • Electric fields
  • Gauss' law
  • Magnetic fields
  • Faraday's law
  • Maxwell equations
  • Special relativity

Reference material

We will follow the Physics 0 lecture notes by Chi-Ming Chang, Qing-Rui Wang, and Fan Yang, distributed in class, together with David Tong's lectures:

  • Classical Dynamics
  • Dynamics and Relativity
  • Electromagnetism
