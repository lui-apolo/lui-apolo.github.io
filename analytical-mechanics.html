Analytical Mechanics

Tsinghua University · Fall 2024

In this class we will cover fundamental aspects of Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, and their relationship to symplectic manifolds.

Schedule

Fridays, 17:05–18:40, Teaching Building 6, Room A305.

Syllabus

  • Variational principles
  • Lagrangian mechanics
  • Conservation laws
  • Hamiltonian mechanics
  • Canonical transformations
  • Action-angle variables
  • The Hamilton-Jacobi equation
  • Differential forms
  • Symplectic manifolds

Reference material

We will follow David Tong's lecture notes on Classical Dynamics supplemented by Goldstein's Classical Mechanics and Arnold's Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics.
